FAQ

Questions brands ask

What does it cost?

Perkalate is free to start. You only pay for the rewards you fund, and you set the budget.

Do I need a loyalty program already?

No. If you have one, Perkalate puts your rewards next to Amex, Delta and Marriott in members' wallets, so more people see and use them. If you don't, Perkalate is the easiest way to start one.

Do I need to build an app or buy hardware?

No. Members use the Perkalate app. You set up rewards in your dashboard, and members redeem with a Pass at the checkout you already have.

How fast can I launch?

You can launch rewards in minutes once your workspace is set up. Most of the work is picking one audience and one reward.

What customer data do I see?

Audiences only. Members choose what they share, the data is permissioned and verified, and you see audience totals and results, never individual identities.
