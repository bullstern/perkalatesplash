# Reach beyond your existing loyalty members

Give new shoppers a path into the program you already run.

Your loyalty program helps you recognize and retain people who already know you. If the goal is to attract a first-time shopper, you need an offer they can discover before they join.

### Give acquisition its own audience

Define one group that already buys in your category but is new to your store. Use information they choose to share, or partner with a business whose audience has a clear reason to try you. Avoid assuming that interest alone predicts a purchase.

### Connect the two experiences

Give the new shopper one reason to try the product. When they redeem, explain one useful reason to join your existing program. Make the second visit or order the next milestone.

### Keep separate measures

Count new customers who used the offer, how many chose to join and how many returned. Compare against a similar cohort. Keep tracking loyalty participation and repeat behavior so you can see which step does the work.
