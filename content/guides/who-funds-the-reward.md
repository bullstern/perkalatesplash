# Who funds the reward and what does it buy?

Define who pays, what counts as a redemption, and how to evaluate the pilot.

A funded reward has a sponsor, a recipient and a specific action. A retailer might fund a gift with a first order. A venue partner might fund a perk that attendees can redeem during one event window. Agree on who pays before the offer goes live.

### Put the terms in writing

Record the eligible audience, the value delivered to each person, the maximum number of redemptions and the total budget. Include fulfillment costs and any fees. Agree on how cancellations, returns and unused rewards are handled.

### Give the sponsor a result it can use

A redemption is useful, but it doesn't establish that the purchase was incremental. Compare a group that received the reward with a similar group that did not. Count the cost of all redeemed rewards and compare it with the contribution from orders above the comparison group's baseline.

### Start with one change

Pick a product or visit that already makes sense for the audience. Run one small test and decide what result would justify a second round. If the offer is easy to explain and the economics are clear, the next decision is easier for everyone.
