# One signal isn’t enough

Combine an eligibility rule with a recent action to make a reward more relevant.

Membership tells you someone joined a program. A recent purchase or visit tells you something about what they do today. A reward can be more relevant when you use two signals that answer different questions.

### Start with a behavior you want to change

Write one sentence describing who should act and what you want them to do. For a first store visit, you might look for shoppers who recently bought in the category and are near the store. A return offer might start with a first purchase and a reasonable reorder window.

### Check that each rule earns its place

Use data the customer has agreed to share. Test the size of the audience after applying each rule. Two signals may be enough. Adding a third is useful only when it improves the audience without making the test too small to measure.

### Compare a simple rule with the combined rule

Give each group the same offer and a fair chance to see it. Compare redemption and repeat behavior. Keep the simplest rule that reliably identifies the people the reward can help.
