# How an online brand can reach shoppers it hasn’t met

Choose one new customer audience and give them a clear reason to try you.

Your own list is a useful place to build repeat business. Reaching first-time shoppers takes a different starting point. Look for people who already buy in your category, then give them one specific reason to try your brand.

### Describe the shopper with observable behavior

Start with the habits your good customers share before they buy. Examples include recent category spend or a relevant membership customers choose to share. Exclude people who already purchase from you if your goal is acquisition.

### Offer a useful first experience

A sample, starter bundle, or shipping perk can remove a specific reason to hesitate. Give the offer a short explanation, a clear eligibility rule, a budget cap and one place to redeem it.

### Measure the first and second order

Track how many eligible people saw, claimed and used the offer. Compare new orders with a similar group that did not receive it. Check whether those buyers return within a sensible window for your product.
