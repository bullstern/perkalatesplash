# Three audience tests for beauty, fashion and wellness

Design a small test with one audience, one offer, and one result to track.

These examples are starting points for planning. Choose signals customers have agreed to share and confirm you can reach enough people before setting the budget.

### Beauty: a useful first appointment

Start with people who recently bought in the category and are within reach of the store. Offer a short product consultation with a relevant sample. Track first visits and follow-on purchases.

### Fashion: a reason to see a new collection

Find shoppers who recently purchased in a related apparel category and have yet to shop with you. Give them an early look or a fitting appointment. Count how many come in and how many return.

### Wellness: a second visit in a quiet slot

Use a first completed class and a window without a second booking. Offer a small perk for an off-peak class that has room. Track attendance and the next booking.

### Compare like with like

Use the same measurement window for the test and comparison groups. Cap redemptions, track fulfillment cost and change one variable after the results come in.
