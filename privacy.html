Gmail privacy supplement

Supplement updated: October 5, 2026

This supplement applies to information received through your optional Gmail connection and takes precedence over conflicting general provisions in the Privacy Policy below. Those general advertising and sharing provisions do not authorize selling Gmail information, sharing it with advertisers, or using it for personalized advertising, brand targeting, offer eligibility or model training.

When you choose to connect Gmail, Google grants read-only mailbox access. Perkalate filters for supported loyalty statements and discount emails from the past 90 days for initial discovery, then incrementally checks new mail with a short overlap, including archived mail and excluding Spam and Trash. This pilot processes message text, not attachments or image-only offers. Unsupported formats and messages over the safe size limit may not produce a result.

Perkalate stores dated extracted information, source references, connection state and encrypted authorization tokens. Complete email bodies are processed transiently rather than stored. This pilot does not send message content to a general-purpose AI processor. Email results are separate from live loyalty-provider balances, connected-value totals and Perkalate reward claims. Current dated membership details and their source references may remain beyond the initial discovery window until replaced by newer statements or you disconnect. Program mentions without membership evidence are labelled as unconfirmed suggestions.

Bookmarking an imported offer saves it for later. It does not claim a reward, confirm redemption or record money saved. Bookmarks are removed when their source is removed during import reconciliation, ages outside the 90-day window, or you disconnect.

Disconnecting stops future imports and deletes active imported information from Perkalate. Perkalate requests revocation of Google access. A separately encrypted token may remain solely for revocation retries, with a seven-day retry deadline. The cleanup worker removes the retry record after confirmed revocation or when it processes an expired record. Pending status or expiration is not proof that Google revoked access. You can also remove access in your Google Account. Disconnecting does not delete Gmail messages or other connected loyalty accounts.

Perkalate’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Information We Collect Through Automatic Data Collection Technologies

As you navigate through and interact with our App, including when you download, access, or use the App, we may use automatic data collection technologies to collect certain information about your device and your browsing and usage activity and patterns, including:

  • Usage Details. When you access and use the App, we may automatically collect certain details of: your access to, use of and interaction with the App; App traffic data; and other communication data and the resources that you access and use on or through the App.

  • Stored Information and Files. The App also may access metadata and other information associated with other files stored on your device. This may include, for example, photographs, audio and video clips, personal contacts, and address book information.

We may retain account-linked place or geofence identifiers, event times, verification results, and related reward activity in accordance with the retention practices described in this policy.

If you do not want us to collect this information do not download the App, delete it from your device, remove our access to those loyalty programs or services you have linked to the App, and remove our ability to access location information through your mobile device settings. For more information, see Your Choices About Our Collection, Use, and Disclosure of Your Information.

Please note that opting out of the App's collection of location information will disable its location-based features. Further, if you remove the App's access to loyalty programs or services, we will be limited in our ability to provide you with offers or other opportunities relating to those loyalty programs or services.

We also may use these technologies to collect information about your activities over time and across third-party websites, apps, or other online services (behavioral tracking). See Your Choices About Our Collection, Use, and Disclosure of Your Information for information on how you can opt out of behavioral tracking on or through this App and how we respond to browser signals and other mechanisms that enable consumers to exercise choice about behavioral tracking.

The technologies we use for automatic information collection may include:

  • Cookies (or mobile cookies). A cookie is a small file placed on your smartphone. It may be possible to refuse to accept mobile cookies by activating the appropriate setting on your smartphone. However, if you select this setting you may be unable to access certain parts of our App.
  • Web Beacons. The App and our emails may contain small electronic files known as web beacons (also referred to as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs) that permit Perkalate, for example, to count users who have visited those pages or opened an email and for other related app statistics (for example, recording the popularity of certain app content and verifying system and server integrity).
  • Session Replay. The App contains technology that allows us to review your interactions with the App. Session replay will capture mouse movements, click targets, scroll positions, and page navigation. If we store a session using session replay, we will remove all personal information, text inputs, passwords, and sensitive form fields. We also do not log keystrokes.
